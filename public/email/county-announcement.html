Monthly Saturday County Training Days at Culford — the vision for 2026/27, and the dates for your diary.
 
Bringing Our County
Programme Together

Dear Players, Parents, Coaches and Clubs,

As the new providers of the Suffolk Performance Pathway, we want to share our vision for the 2026/27 season — and it starts with bringing the county together.

From September, our 11U–18U county squads will train together at monthly Saturday afternoon County Training Days at Culford, using all six indoor courts across the Main Indoor Centre and the Bubble.

ONE COUNTY.
ONE PROGRAMME.
Every age group. Every squad. One Saturday. One venue.
The Saturday Structure
Culford — All Six Indoor Courts
12.00 – 2.00pm
Main Indoor 11U & 12U Girls
Bubble 14U Girls
2.00 – 4.00pm
Main Indoor 12U & 14U Boys
Bubble 11U Boys
4.00 – 6.00pm
Main Indoor & Bubble 18U Boys & Girls

16U Girls train separately at another location  ·  10U operates separately  ·  8U & 9U are moving towards weekly training — details to follow.

What Happens Next?

You’ll receive a personalised email confirming your training group — age group, time, location, County Captain(s) and coaches — followed by squads, invitations, booking and payment information through the new Suffolk Tennis booking system.

What Training Together Means
● A stronger connection across the whole pathway
● Younger players seeing what they can aspire to
● Older players becoming role models
● Coaches and captains working across age groups
● A real Suffolk Tennis atmosphere on training days
● Every player understanding where they are — and where they can go
● Players proud to represent Suffolk
