Suffolk Tennis · 10U Pathway
We want you involved

Hi,

I wanted to get in touch to introduce the direction we are taking with Suffolk Tennis, and in particular our plans to significantly strengthen the 10U pathway and Talent ID network across the county.

Alongside Danny Wyatt, Lead Coach for our 10U Enhanced Performance Programme at Culford, and Chris Daynes, our 11–18 County Lead, I have recently taken responsibility for the 10U performance provision as Suffolk’s 10U County Lead.

One of our biggest priorities is simple: to reconnect Suffolk Tennis with the coaches, clubs and schools developing young players every single week.

You are doing the most important part of the job

You are putting rackets in children’s hands, building their skills, inspiring them to love the game, and often seeing their potential long before they appear on the county radar.

Our job is to connect that work into a clear Suffolk pathway. We want to know who your players are and who the coaches developing them are, and we want to create additional opportunities for those children without taking them away from the environments and people responsible for their development.

Talent ID
Is there someone we should know about?

Our immediate Talent ID focus is on children born in 2020 and 2021, and those coming behind them, while continuing to support our 2019 cohort towards 8U County Cup next year. These days are the start of the County Rising Stars programme for 8U players: the first step on the Suffolk pathway, there to support each child’s tennis journey from the very beginning.

We would like every coach, club and school receiving this to look at the children you work with and ask that question. They don’t need to be an established county player. We’re interested in children who catch your eye through their athletic ability, coordination, competitiveness, character, enthusiasm, love of sport, or simply that little something that makes you think there could be potential.

If you’re unsure, nominate them.
We’d rather see a child than miss one.

Coaches
We want coaches involved too

This isn’t only about identifying players. Are you a coach who would like to become more involved with the Suffolk pathway?

We want to build a much wider network of coaches across the county: share ideas, create opportunities, and better connect the great work already happening within clubs, schools and coaching programmes.

We also want to recognise it. Through our new website we will increasingly showcase the clubs, schools and coaches contributing to Suffolk’s player pathway, and give parents a clearer picture of the people and programmes developing tennis across our county.

suffolktennis.online
A more connected Suffolk pathway

We’ve launched suffolktennis.online to start bringing all of this together: the beginning of a new digital home for Suffolk Tennis, connecting the pathway, county training, Talent ID, competition, clubs, coaches and information for parents in one place.

We’re also introducing a new digital platform for families. Following nominations, parents receive digital invitations and sign-ups directly through the platform, making county opportunities much simpler to access and giving families a clearer connection to their child’s journey.
